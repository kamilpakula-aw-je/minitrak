Tigre 3200 - Isodiametric steering compact tractor
DON'T CALL IT A MINI TRACTOR

The most compact model in the Antonio Carraro range, the Tigre 3200 occupies an important place in the history of the marque. The latest in a long line of compact tractors that for decades have earned the appreciation of professional farmers and enthusiasts.
An isodiametric all-rounder, originally designed and continuously improved to satisfy multiple operating requirements. It is perfect for use in parks, gardens, sports grounds, public areas and agricultural smallholdings.
Powered by 3-cylinder 26 hp Yanmar engine, it combines simplicity with performance and economy.

The Tigre stands out for its reliability, comfort and safety, these being the essential criteria for both professionals and enthusiasts. The outstanding versatility of this tractor enables operation in widest range of environments, 365 days a year.

Choose a Tigre and make a friend for life.
