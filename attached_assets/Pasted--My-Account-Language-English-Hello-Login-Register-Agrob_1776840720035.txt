Arbos / AUPAX 3075
AUPAX 3075 – a powerful and versatile farm workhorse
AUPAX 3075 is a 75 HP category tractor designed for a wide range of agricultural tasks. It is ideally suited for medium-sized farms and various agricultural sectors – from soil cultivation and seeding to orchard care and farm transport. With its robust 4WD system and increased ground clearance, AUPAX 3075 performs reliably in dry fields, wet soils, and hilly terrain where traction is essential. Its top speed of up to 40 km/h ensures quick travel between fields or transporting loads to the farmyard. This is a truly multi-purpose machine – one tractor that can replace several specialized machines across your operation.


Compared to smaller AUPAX models (e.g., the 25 HP AUPAX 2025 or 40 HP AUPAX 2040), the 3075 stands out with significantly greater power and productivity. Its 4-cylinder Doosan engine (approx. 2.4 L displacement) delivers 75 HP and an impressive 320 Nm of torque at just 1600 rpm – more than double the torque of the 40 HP model. This enables AUPAX 3075 to pull heavier implements and work larger fields with ease, where smaller tractors would struggle. Its 2600 kg lifting capacity far exceeds the ~800 kg capacity of compact models, making it capable of handling a much broader and heavier range of implements such as ploughs, cultivators, and front loaders.
AUPAX 3075 truly is the “workhorse” of the farm, built to handle demanding tasks and replace multiple machines with a single, reliable solution.
