Arbos / AUPAX 2040
AUPAX 2040 – a universal solution for farming and municipal work
Versatile performance
AUPAX 2040 is a compact 40 HP tractor designed for both small farms and a wide range of municipal applications. With a width of only 1.65 meters, it easily maneuvers in tight spaces, making it ideal for orchards, vineyards, greenhouses, and other small agricultural areas.

This model also proves invaluable for urban and community maintenance – from park care to snow removal and material transport.
AUPAX 2040 can handle numerous municipal tasks: mowing green areas, clearing sidewalks or parking lots with a snow blade, spreading sand or salt, sweeping streets, or assisting with loading operations using a trailer or front loader.

With a top speed of around 30 km/h, it ensures efficient travel between work sites and quick transport of materials over short distances.
It’s a truly universal helper that enhances daily efficiency both in the farmyard and municipal environment.


AUPAX 2040 is an investment in reliability, comfort, and versatility.
This tractor will help you increase productivity, reduce operating costs, and simplify daily operations, allowing you to focus on what matters most – getting the job done efficiently.
