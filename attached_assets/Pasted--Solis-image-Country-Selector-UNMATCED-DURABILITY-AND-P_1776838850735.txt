Exceptional Excellence Enduring Dependability
Solis S50 delivers exceptional performance, engineered to maximize output with the toughness of a true farming professional. Designed for all-weather versatility, it is fully equipped with user-friendly, comfortable features to ensure ease of use in any climate. With a high backup torque and a lifting capacity of up to 1600 kg, this tractor is built for heavy-duty tasks.
